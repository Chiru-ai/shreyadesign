About Shilpasaurbha — Preserving Aari & Heritage Crafts

Founded by master artisan Shreya Mehta, our studio is a sanctuary for India’s finest Aari embroidery and indigenous handicrafts. We collaborate with over 80 rural women artisans, empowering them with fair wages and creative freedom. Every Aari piece involves delicate hook work, taking 40 to 200 hours of devotion. Our handicraft collection ranges from Dhokra metalwork to handwoven textiles — each item carries the soul of Indian tradition.

We believe in sustainable luxury, ethical sourcing, and timeless design that transcends seasons.
