About Shreya Designer Studio — Preserving Aari & Heritage Crafts

Founded by Shreya, our studio is a sanctuary for India’s finest Aari embroidery and indigenous handicrafts. Every Aari piece involves delicate hook work, taking 40 to 200 hours of devotion. Our handicraft collection ranges from Dhokra metalwork to handwoven textiles — each item carries the soul of Indian tradition.

We believe in sustainable luxury, ethical sourcing, and timeless design that transcends seasons. From bespoke bridal lehengas to artisan home decor, Shreya Designer Studio brings your vision to life with unmatched craftsmanship.
